Security+ for Product Designers — CompTIA SY0-801

One 16-week self-paced pathway with two outcomes: readiness for the Security+ exam and the ability to apply security knowledge as a Senior Product Designer. It has four weeks of IT and networking foundations, nine weeks across the five exam domains, and three weeks of revision and integrated case studies. Design exercises sit inside the lessons where the concept has a real design application. Mock exams, performance-based practice and progress tracking are in the interactive app. Objective numbers are shown with short names in Atlas's own words. The exact wording is in CompTIA's objectives document.
