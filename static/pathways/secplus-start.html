Pathway: Security+ for Product Designers — CompTIA SY0-801 · Lesson 1 · Basics · about 15 min

This pathway has its own pages for lessons, labs, design exercises, knowledge checks, mock exams and two separate progress measures.

By the end, you can: Open the pathway, understand how it tracks technical mastery and design competency separately, and decide whether its AI-written material suits you.

Why it matters to a designer

Each security concept is connected directly to how it appears in products and how you would design for it, so exam preparation and professional practice build on each other.

Key ideas

  • The first four weeks are foundations and are not exam domains.
  • Each lesson has six parts: learn the concept, technical deep dive, see it in real products, apply it as a Product Designer, test your understanding, then review.
  • Security+ mastery and design competency are measured separately. Neither can raise the other.
  • The practice questions are AI-written and are not authorized by CompTIA. Read CompTIA's policy on the pathway's first page before relying on them.

Key terms
