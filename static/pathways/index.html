Pathways

The 60-day calendar is Atlas's core: security foundations, identity, access, zero trust, sign-in and governance. Pathways are focused tracks beside it, to go deeper in one area alongside the calendar or after it.

Network and Kubernetes Security

Containers, Kubernetes networking and network policy, hands-on with a local cluster and Calico, and case study 2: a flow from observed traffic to a recommended policy, staged before it is enforced. These are Week 5 and the case study 2 days of the 60-day calendar, grouped here for learners going deep on cloud network security.

For: Designers targeting network, cloud or Kubernetes security companies such as Tigera. If identity or AI is your focus, you can make these days optional in Settings → Content and use the AI Security pathway's practice lesson as your second case study.

AI Security

How AI products and agents fail, and how to design them so they cannot exceed the authority a person gave them: trust boundaries in AI systems, direct and indirect prompt injection, data exposure through retrieval and memory, excessive agency and tool permissions, supply chain, output handling, monitoring and incident response. Ends with a designed agent permission and approval experience.

For: Designers aiming at AI security, identity for agents or any product that connects AI to enterprise data. Take Days 22, 50 and 51 first, and Days 8 (clients, servers and APIs) and 11 (when access control fails).
