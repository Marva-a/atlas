Case study 2 draft: write it up and share

Day 55 · Week 8 · Advanced · about 120 min

You will write the full draft of case study 2 and compare both case studies against real job posts.

By the end, you can: Write a first draft of the network-policy case study.

Why it matters to a designer

With two drafts, you can now see your portfolio as a whole. Matching it against real roles at companies like Tailscale and Tigera shows what to emphasise in Week 9 and in interviews.

Key ideas

  • Use the same structure as case study 1: problem, role, research, concepts, design, testing, outcome.
  • Explain the technical problem simply: one picture of default allow versus least privilege in a cluster.
  • Show your rollout thinking: staged, previewed, reversible. This is a senior design skill.
  • Read two real job posts and map each required skill to evidence in your case studies. Gaps become Week 9 tasks.
  • Share the draft with one practitioner with two specific questions.

Key terms

skills mapA table linking job requirements to evidence in your work.
gapA skill a job asks for that your portfolio does not yet show.

Check yourself

  • Which skill from the job posts does your portfolio show most strongly?
  • What is your biggest gap, and how will you address it in Week 9?

What a good answer looks like

  • A good answer names a specific skill (for example designing for technical users or turning research into design) and the exact part of a case study that proves it.
  • A good answer names the gap honestly and a concrete action, for example adding a metrics section, showing collaboration with engineers or preparing a story for interviews.
