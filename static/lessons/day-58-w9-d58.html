Polish case study 2: story, outcome, honest labels

Day 58 · Week 9 · Part of the Network and Kubernetes Security pathway · Advanced · about 120 min

You will finish case study 2 to the same standard, making the technical subject easy to follow for any reader.

By the end, you can: Revise the network-policy case study's story and outcome with accurate, honestly labelled claims.

Why it matters to a designer

Your second case study proves range: a different user (the platform engineer), a deeper technical domain and a rollout problem. Together the two pieces tell hiring managers you can work across identity and network security.

Key ideas

  • Apply the Week 8 feedback and record the changes.
  • Explain Kubernetes network policy in one picture and one paragraph, for readers who do not know it.
  • Highlight what is unique: staged enforcement and trust in recommendations.
  • Show collaboration: what you learned from engineers and how it changed the design.
  • End with outcome and next steps: metrics, open questions and what you would test next.
  • Check consistency between the two case studies: structure, labels, image style.

Key terms

rangeShowing you can solve different kinds of problems.
consistencyUsing the same structure and style across pieces so readers can compare.

What a good answer looks like

  • A good answer is tested: someone outside the field read the opening and could explain the problem back in their own words.
  • For example: designing for a highly technical user, working with traffic data and recommendations, and designing a safe staged rollout in Kubernetes.
