Portfolio page, LinkedIn and resume for identity and security roles

Day 59 · Week 9 · Advanced · about 120 min

You will present yourself as a product designer for identity and network security: portfolio home page, LinkedIn and resume, all telling the same story.

By the end, you can: Write a portfolio page, LinkedIn summary and resume section positioned for identity/security product-design roles.

Why it matters to a designer

Recruiters and hiring managers at companies like Tailscale and Tigera search for specific signals: security domain knowledge, technical users, complex systems. Your materials should show these at a glance, backed by your two case studies.

Key ideas

  • Your positioning sentence: who you design for and what you solve, for example “Product designer for identity and network security: I make access and infrastructure tools clear and safe.”
  • Portfolio home: the positioning sentence, the two case studies first, then other work.
  • LinkedIn: headline with your positioning, an About section with your story into the domain, and featured links to both case studies.
  • Resume: lead with relevant outcomes; add the domain skills you can now honestly claim (zero trust, SSO and SCIM, Kubernetes network policy, security UX).
  • Use real job posts: mirror their language where it is true for you.
  • Be honest about what you learned in this course and what you did in past roles. Specific and true beats impressive and vague.

Key terms

positioningA short statement of who you help and how.
headlineThe line under your name on LinkedIn.
featuredLinkedIn’s section for highlighting links such as case studies.
