Exercise 10: redesign the SSO and SCIM setup flow

Day 26 · Week 4 · Intermediate · about 120 min

You will redesign the flow an admin follows to connect their company’s identity provider to a product like Tailscale, with SSO and SCIM, so it is guided and checkable.

By the end, you can: Redesign an enterprise SSO and SCIM setup flow to reduce admin friction.

Why it matters to a designer

Enterprise setup flows decide whether a trial turns into a sale. They are long, span two admin consoles, and fail with cryptic errors. This is a typical senior design problem in identity companies, and a strong portfolio piece.

Key ideas

  • Map the real setup first: open two vendor guides (for example WorkOS and Microsoft Entra) and list every step, value and decision.
  • Most pain comes from copying values between two products, from not knowing if a step worked, and from errors that only appear at the end.
  • Design ideas that work: a checklist with progress, copy buttons, validating each value as it is pasted, a “test connection” step, and a clear success state.
  • Wizards help for long, rare tasks done by non-experts, but experts want to skip ahead. Offer both.
  • Remember SCIM: after SSO works, the admin must decide which groups to sync and what happens to people who leave. Show the effect in plain words: “12 people will be added; 3 will be removed”.

Key terms

setup flowThe steps an admin follows to connect two products.
wizardA step-by-step guided flow for a long task.
validationChecking an input as it is entered.
test connectionA step that proves the setup works before going live.
preview of effectShowing what will happen before the admin confirms it.

Check yourself

  • Where does most pain in SSO setup come from?
  • How does your design tell the admin a step worked?

What a good answer looks like

  • From copying values between two consoles, not knowing if each step worked, and errors that appear only at the end with unclear messages.
  • For example by validating values as they are pasted, a “test connection” step with a clear result, and a success screen that says what now works and what to do next.
