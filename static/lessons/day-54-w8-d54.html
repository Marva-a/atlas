Case study 2: test and improve

Day 54 · Week 8 · Advanced · about 120 min

You will test your traffic-to-policy prototype with two engineers and improve it based on what you see.

By the end, you can: Test the network-policy prototype with a participant and refine it based on what they actually did.

Why it matters to a designer

Testing with technical users shows whether your design is not just clear but credible. Engineers will tell you quickly if something would not work in a real cluster, and that feedback makes your case study trustworthy.

Key ideas

  • Task example: “The payments namespace has no policies. Use the tool to protect it without breaking checkout.”
  • Watch for credibility: do they trust the recommendations? Do they check the YAML? What would make them enforce?
  • Engineers often ask “what about…?” questions. Note each one; they are edge cases your design may need.
  • Fix the most important problems today, and note the rest as next steps.
  • Compare with case study 1 testing: what was different about testing with engineers versus admins?

Key terms

credibilityWhether expert users believe a design would work in real life.
participantA person taking part in a test.
next stepsWork you would do with more time, stated in the case study.

Check yourself

  • What did engineers question most in your design?
  • How was testing with engineers different from testing with admins?

What a good answer looks like

  • A good answer names the specific doubt (for example how much traffic a recommendation is based on) and what you changed to address it.
  • For example: engineers checked the real configuration and asked about edge cases, while admins focused on understanding the effect. Both shaped the design differently.
