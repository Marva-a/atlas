Interview practice: a live design exercise and a retrospective

Day 60 · Week 9 · Advanced · about 120 min

You will practise the interviews you will face: presenting a case study, a live design exercise in this domain, and a retrospective on the 60 days.

By the end, you can: Explain a security architecture in plain language, identify a risk in a live design exercise, and defend a design trade-off under questioning.

Why it matters to a designer

Senior interviews test how you think out loud, handle technical questions and take feedback. Practising with domain-specific prompts turns what you learned into confident answers.

Key ideas

  • Case study presentation: 15 minutes, problem first, then key decisions and trade-offs, then results. Leave time for questions.
  • Live exercise prompts to practise: “Design how an admin finds out why an employee cannot reach a server” or “Design how an engineer rolls out a network policy safely”.
  • In live exercises, ask clarifying questions first (who, what scale, what constraints), then think aloud, sketch, and name trade-offs.
  • Expect domain questions: explain zero trust, SSO versus SCIM, default deny, or how an agent should be authorised. Your glossary is your study guide.
  • Retrospective: what you can do now that you could not on Day 1, what is still weak, and your next 30 days.

Key terms

design exerciseA live or take-home design problem used in interviews.
clarifying questionA question that narrows the problem before you start.
retrospectiveA look back at what went well, what did not, and what to change.

What a good answer looks like

  • Clarifying questions: who the user is, what they are trying to do, at what scale, under which constraints and how success is measured. Then state your assumptions.
  • A good answer is specific, for example explaining zero trust and network policy in plain words, designing for admins and engineers, and presenting two case studies with evidence.
